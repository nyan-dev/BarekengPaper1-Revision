The manuscript presents a comparative study between Isolation Forest and Local Outlier Factor for anomaly detection using healthcare data. The structure, methodology, and experimental results are generally well-organized and clear.
However, to meet the scope of BAREKENG: Journal of Mathematics and Its Applications, several major revisions are required:
Strengthen mathematical aspects
The paper is currently more application-oriented (machine learning). It should include deeper mathematical or statistical analysis, such as theoretical justification, formulation, or analytical discussion of the algorithms. 
Improve depth of analysis
The discussion is mostly descriptive. More rigorous statistical and mathematical interpretation is needed (e.g., interpretation of Jaccard Index, distribution analysis). 
Clarify novelty/contribution
The contribution compared to existing studies should be explicitly highlighted. 
Methodological justification
Provide justification for parameter selection (e.g., contamination value) and include sensitivity analysis if possible. 
Minor issues 
Improve academic writing and grammar 
Ensure consistency of terminology 
Complete missing sections (Author Contributions, Funding, etc.)
